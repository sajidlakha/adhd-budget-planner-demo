Polite message templates - copy, add your details, send

Fee back: Hello, I noticed a fee on my account. I would like to understand what it was for, and whether it can be reviewed or refunded as a goodwill gesture. Thank you for your help.

Cancel a subscription: Hello, please cancel my subscription from today and confirm in writing that no further payments will be taken. Please also confirm the last payment date. Thank you.

Lower a bill: Hello, I have been a customer for some time and I am reviewing my monthly costs. Is there a lower plan, discount or loyalty offer available for my account? Thank you for checking.

Oops helper - three small steps

1. Breathe and write down what happened, in one sentence.

2. Fix the next small thing only: pay the bill, call the bank, or move the money.

3. Add one guard for next time: a reminder, autopay, or a separate account for that bill.

A late fee, an overdraft, a forgotten bill or a regret buy does not erase your progress. It is one event, not a verdict.

52-week challenge

Week 1 saves $1, week 2 saves $2, and so on. By week 52 you have saved $1,378 at a $1 base. If you miss a week, just do the next one. The total still grows.
